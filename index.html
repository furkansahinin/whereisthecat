HELLO, FELLOW WANDERER

One city. Many lives.
A memory passed from cat to cat.

Our cat knows the shopkeepers, the forgotten stages and the buildings that changed their names. The old street cats pass their stories down. We use that imagined voice to lead you toward real, sourced histories.

Each stop begins with an imagined cat scene, then opens into documented history and sources. A historical trace may mark a lost place rather than a surviving venue. Our selection follows people, work and memory, rather than popularity. Enjoy wandering, and don't forget to say hello to the stray cats along the way.
